# Milestone 0 — Executive summary

**MRR is $246,739; paid churn runs ~2.6% of customers and ~1.6% of revenue
per month; and the single most leveraged action is a day-30 CS trigger, not
a product redesign.**

1. **The foundation (M2).** Active paid MRR is $246,739 — enterprise $168,373
   (68%), pro $69,684, starter $8,682 — with 12-month averages of 2.64%
   customer churn and 1.62% gross revenue churn on a churn base of active +
   past-due subscriptions (paused excluded, matching M3). The base doubled in
   a year ($129k → $263k); a Mar–May 2026 uptick (3.1/2.9/3.9%) needs
   explaining. Books reconcile within $513 (0.1%).
2. **The shape (M3).** Retention is decided in the first 30 days: ~38% of each
   cohort is gone by month 2, then the curve never decays — and the 2026
   uptick spans 29 cohorts, so no single bad cohort is dragging the average.
3. **The trigger (M4, escalated first).** Companies with first-month
   dashboard/export activity but no teammate invite churn at 36.2% vs ~21%
   baseline (1.7×, n=58) — the only signal both large and immediately
   triggerable. CS runs the watchlist now (18 companies live today, ~3 extra
   saves per monthly cohort); product's onboarding redesign follows, measured
   against this segment. Sequencing, not hedging: M4 first for speed, M3 for
   scale.
